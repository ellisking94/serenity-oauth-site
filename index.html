PRIVATE WORKFLOW · MEDIA DELIVERY

A thoughtful way to deliver original Serenity content.

Serenity Knowledge Automation is an owner-operated utility that connects the owner's Google Drive media folder to an authorised social publishing workflow.

What the connector does

After the owner authorises access, the connector can upload completed and approved social media images and videos to the existing Serenity Knowledge Google Drive media folder, verify that approved media can be retrieved and make the appropriate media available to the owner's Buffer publishing account.

The connector is intended for the account owner's private use. There is no public account registration, community feature or third-party marketing service.

It does not grant visitors access to the owner's Google Drive. Only explicitly approved media intended for social publishing may be made publicly downloadable.

Owner controlled

Google Drive authorisation is granted by the account owner and can be revoked through the owner's Google Account settings.

Restricted purpose

Google Drive access is used for the media hosting and publishing workflow. The application is not intended to search, profile or sell private Drive content.
